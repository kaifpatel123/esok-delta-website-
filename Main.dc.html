About the Club

A holistic space for economics at Krea.

Through discussion, collaboration and exploration of diverse schools of thought, the Club encourages a holistic understanding of economic issues, connecting ideas across disciplines. It is also a platform for networking and mentorship across cohorts, with scholars and with industry professionals.
