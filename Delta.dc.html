What is Delta?

A flagship academic and cultural event.

Delta is Krea University's flagship economics fest, presented by the Economics Club. It brings together diverse perspectives on the forces that shape, influence and redirect economic systems.

It is a platform for learning and collaboration across disciplines, where theory meets practice and students engage directly with thought leaders, practitioners and innovators.

The first edition, themed "A World in Crisis: Everything Everywhere All at Once", ran on 21 and 22 March 2026. Delta 26–27 returns with a new theme.

Theme 26–27

Ex Machina: The Power Behind the System

The name comes from deus ex machina, an outside force entering a difficult situation and changing its outcome. Here the "machine" is the economy: markets, governments, financial institutions, firms and digital platforms. The "power behind the system" is the people, institutions, rules, technologies and incentives that redirect outcomes.

No intervention is completely neutral. A regulation may protect consumers and raise costs; automation may lift productivity and cut certain jobs; a bailout may prevent a collapse and encourage future risk. The theme asks who ultimately bears the cost of a decision.
