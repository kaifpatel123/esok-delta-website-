Events at Krea

Panel discussion

Why Does the Economy Feel Broken When the Numbers Look Good?

A panel discussion with Dr. Vidya Bharti Rajkumar and Dr. Suchita Chopra, exploring the disconnect between favourable macroeconomic indicators and the economic realities experienced by individuals. The conversation examined the limitations of conventional economic metrics in capturing lived experiences and challenged students to question prevailing economic narratives.

This was followed by a discussion on the Economics Capstone Thesis at Krea University, highlighting its research framework, academic expectations, and the process of developing an independent research project.

Interactive evening

Economics Club Mixer

An interactive, auction-themed evening featuring Jeopardy, Pictionary and Taboo, bringing economics to life through fun and engaging challenges. Participants used event tokens as virtual currency to bid, compete and test their knowledge across themes like Box Office Economics, Sports Economics and the Game of Loans, with winning teams rewarded with Narsi coupons.
