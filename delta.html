The theme

Who is behind
the machine?

The name comes from deus ex machina, an outside force entering a difficult situation and changing its outcome. Here the “machine” is the economy; the “power behind the system” is the people, institutions, rules, technologies and incentives that redirect outcomes.

Influence markets through taxes, subsidies, regulation and public spending.
