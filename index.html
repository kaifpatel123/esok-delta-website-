About the Club

A holistic space
for economics at Krea.

The Krea Economics Club is a student community where economic ideas meet real-world conversation. We connect students, alumni, and industry professionals through talks, discussions, and events like our fest Delta 1.0. As a platform for informed dialogue, we offer perspectives on policy, markets, and current affairs without pushing any single viewpoint. Our aim is to help members understand how economics shapes everyday decisions and to build lasting networks along the way. Whether you’re an economics major or simply curious, there’s a place for you here.

01
Understanding

Promote a better understanding of economics

  • Prioritise education and co-creation of knowledge through a forum for critical engagement.
  • Expose members to schools of thought beyond the mainstream.
  • Evolve economics at Krea through “Holistic Economics”.
  • Recognise the discipline’s epistemic limits and learn from other disciplines.
02
Networking

Provide a platform that enables networking

  • Connect students interested in economics across all cohorts.
  • Bring students, scholars and industry experts together.
  • Build a mentorship programme for incoming batches.
  • Create a system for Teaching and Research Assistantships.
03
Suggestive body

Act as a suggestive body

  • Engage with issues on or off campus through an intra- and interdisciplinary lens.
  • Help shape guidelines, plans and policies directed at those issues.
What we do

Initiatives and activities

01

A forum for ideas

Co-creating knowledge through critical engagement and a free exchange of thoughts among members.

02

Holistic Economics

Exploring economics beyond the syllabus, through discussions that bring its many fields and perspectives together.

03

Networking

Connecting students across cohorts with scholars and industry experts, so conversations continue well beyond the classroom.

04

Mentorship

Helping incoming batches find their footing in the rigour of economics at Krea, with guidance from those who have been through it.

05

Student opportunities

A system for opportunities such as Teaching and Research Assistantships.

06

A suggestive body

Engaging with campus and outside issues, and helping shape plans and policies.
