About the Club

A holistic space
for economics at Krea.

A student-led initiative promoting critical engagement with economics through discussion, collaboration and diverse schools of thought, with room for networking and mentorship across cohorts, scholars and industry professionals.

01
Understanding

Promote a better understanding of economics

  • Prioritise education and co-creation of knowledge through a forum for critical engagement.
  • Expose members to schools of thought beyond the mainstream.
  • Evolve economics at Krea through “Holistic Economics”.
  • Recognise the discipline’s epistemic limits and learn from other disciplines.
02
Networking

Provide a platform that enables networking

  • Connect students interested in economics across all cohorts.
  • Bring students, scholars and industry experts together.
  • Build a mentorship programme for incoming batches.
  • Create a system for Teaching and Research Assistantships.
03
Suggestive body

Act as a suggestive body

  • Engage with issues on or off campus through an intra- and interdisciplinary lens.
  • Help shape guidelines, plans and policies directed at those issues.
